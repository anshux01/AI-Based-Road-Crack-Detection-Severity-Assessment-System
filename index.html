BUSINESS UNDERSTANDING
❗ Problem Statement

Business Problem

Client: Municipal Road Authority
Road surface cracks — longitudinal, transverse, alligator, and edge cracks — are often detected late through manual inspections or public complaints.

Consequences of Late Detection

  • Progressive pavement deterioration
  • Water infiltration into road layers
  • Structural weakening of pavements
  • High full-depth repair costs
⚠️ Inconsistent severity assessment further results in poor prioritization of maintenance activities.
The project focuses more on accurate crack region highlighting rather than only class prediction.

CHALLENGES
⚠️ Challenges Faced

Challenges

⚖️

Class Imbalance

Significant imbalance across crack types — alligator cracks were overrepresented while edge cracks had far fewer samples. Addressed through targeted augmentation and class weighting during training.

🔍

Missing Edge Crack Data

Edge cracks were underrepresented in publicly available datasets. Custom annotation was required using Label Studio on additional collected images to supplement training data.

✏️

Annotation Complexity

Precise polygon annotation for thin and irregular cracks is time-consuming and prone to human error. Overlapping crack patterns required careful manual review and correction.

🌧️

Real-world Noise

Images captured in varying conditions — shadows, rain, debris, oil stains — reduce detection accuracy. Robust preprocessing and augmentation were applied to improve model generalization.
